# CareTrail image assets

Created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on October 25, 2026, then compressed to responsive WebP assets. These are fictional illustrative scenes selected to match the relief, medical-care, and education subjects in the supplied Figma design. They are not exported Figma photographs, actual beneficiaries, or evidence of campaign outcomes.

Files: campaign-relief.webp, campaign-medical.webp, campaign-education.webp; each has a -small.webp variant. Large versions are 1200 pixels wide; small versions are 640 pixels wide. Images are used in campaign cards/detail pages. Relief is also used on the landing page and authentication screens.

Prompt set:

- Relief: photorealistic 3:2 editorial scene of a Filipino adult volunteer in a cobalt shirt arranging relief boxes outdoors in a tropical community; natural morning light; hopeful, dignified composition; no text or logos; explicitly fictional sample imagery.
- Medical: photorealistic 3:2 editorial close-up of a healthcare worker's hands, a stethoscope and consultation desk in a pale-blue clinic; soft daylight; no identifiable patient or distress; no text or logos; fictional illustration.
- Education: photorealistic 3:2 editorial school still life with notebooks, pencils, books and a cobalt backpack on a wooden table beside a tropical window; warm daylight; no people, text or logos; fictional illustration.

Replace category illustrations with campaign-owned, authorized photographs when real campaign imagery is available. Keep the label and receipt/report distinction accurate.
